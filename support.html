Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a teleconsultation? +

To book a teleconsultation, go to "Find Doctors" from your dashboard, select a doctor, choose your preferred date and time, and confirm your booking. You'll receive a confirmation with a link to join the video call.

What equipment do I need for a video consultation? +

You'll need a smartphone, tablet, or computer with a camera and microphone. Make sure you have a stable internet connection and are in a quiet, well-lit area for the best experience.

Is my medical information secure? +

Yes, we take your privacy seriously. All consultations are encrypted, and your medical information is stored securely. We comply with healthcare data protection standards.

How do I cancel or reschedule an appointment? +

You can cancel or reschedule appointments from your dashboard up to 2 hours before the scheduled time. Go to "Upcoming Appointments" and select the appointment you wish to modify.

What payment methods are accepted? +

We accept mobile money (Orange Money, Africell Money), bank transfers, and cash payments at partner facilities. Payment details are provided when you book an appointment.
